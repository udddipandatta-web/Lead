# Product knowledge: Finely Tossed

## What we sell

Finely Tossed, run by Uddipan Datta, makes bespoke **Digital Culinary Portfolios** and **Interactive Digital Tasting Menus** for Michelin-starred and fine-dining chefs. Each one tells the story of a chef's menu course by course: the philosophy, the sourcing, the technique and the people behind it. Guests can explore it before they arrive and revisit it afterwards.

Examples to point to (one link per email at most):
- https://tastingmenu.netlify.app
- https://uddipan-datta-culinary.netlify.app

## Who it is for

Chefs and owners of Michelin-starred, Michelin-recommended and serious tasting-menu restaurants in the UK, Ireland and the US. The best fits publish their menu as plain text or a PDF, or not at all, and have a story worth telling: a new star, a new opening, a signature dish, a distinctive sourcing philosophy.

## Rules for every email and reply (these override style advice elsewhere)

1. **Never mention price**, cost, fees, rates, budgets, discounts, or anything free (no "free preview", "free audit", "no cost"). If a prospect asks about price, say it is bespoke and offer a short call.
2. **Never use the words** "website", "SEO", "digital marketing", "web development", "web design", "agency", "traffic", "conversions" or "rankings". Talk about the menu, the dishes, the story, the guest experience and the craft.
3. **Open with a personal hook** about this chef: a recent star or award, a named signature dish, the menu's structure, an opening, a sourcing story. If you have no specific, verifiable detail, do not write the email.
4. **Low-friction call to action**: offer to sketch a short concept around one of their menus and ask if they would be open to taking a look at a preview. Do not ask for a meeting in the first email.
5. Tone: warm, refined and respectful of the chef's craft. Write as one craftsman to another. No hype, no exclamation marks.
6. Sign as Uddipan Datta, Finely Tossed.

## Buying triggers

- A new Michelin star, Green Star or Bib Gourmand, or a new entry in the Guide
- A new restaurant opening or relocation
- A new seasonal or extended tasting menu
- A menu that is only a PDF, an image, a list of course names, or missing online
- Press coverage of a signature dish or the chef's story

## Disqualify

- Casual dining, chains, cafés, takeaways and hotel groups' generic dining
- Any restaurant already contacted (they are marked "contacted" in Harvey's database)
- Anyone who asked not to be contacted
